## Editorial boundaries

- Do not publish a combined AI-capex total until the company universe, fiscal
  period and accounting treatment are reconstructible.
- Do not treat a sharp one-day selloff, an implied volatility reading or a flow
  headline as evidence of capitulation without a source-dated and reproducible
  series.
- Do not equate supplier revenue with a purchaser's realised return on invested
  capital.
