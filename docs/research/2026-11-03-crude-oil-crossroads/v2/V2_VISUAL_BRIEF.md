# Crude Oil Visual Briefing V2

## Purpose

V2 is the approved published form of the oil note. It rebuilds the reading
experience around short sections, visible evidence and explicit uncertainty
labels.

## Route and containment

- **Canonical route:** `/research/crude-oil-crossroads-brent-curve-physical-markets`
- **Status:** `draft: false`, indexable and public.
- **Hub and sitemap:** included.
- **Publication:** approved by the owner on 4 November 2026.

## What changed

1. **Verified flow-bar chart**
   Uses IEA October 2026 reported September figures: Gulf producer crude
   exports at 13.9 mb/d, Hormuz flows at 10.9 mb/d and pipeline bypass flows
   at 5.6 mb/d. The figure explicitly states that the measures overlap and must
   not be added.
2. **Signal board**
   Separates observed flows and inventory context from the non-reproducible
   combined positioning percentile and unverified diplomacy narrative.
3. **Brent spread explainer**
   Shows the two legs of the historical Sep 2027 versus Dec 2027 structure and
   the relative-value condition for a wider or narrower spread. It includes no
   settlement chart, trade call or invented performance result.
4. **Scenario cards and Malaysia transmission grid**
   Replaces a dense table with scannable evidence triggers while retaining the
   distinction between Brent, USD/MYR and FCPO channels.

## Source and visual boundary

The same source register applies as V1. V2 does not reuse Barclays screenshots,
charts, chart data, logo or layout. It uses only the independently cited IEA
flow values as quantitative bars; all other visual elements are explanatory
Aeora frameworks.

## Review criteria

- Is the source boundary still clear while the reading experience is faster?
- Do the bar labels and caveat prevent readers from adding overlapping flow measures?
- Does the spread diagram explain relative-value risk without becoming a signal?
- Is the V2 visual hierarchy preferable to the V1 long-form note?
