## Internal-linking rules

1. Each pillar links to its two most relevant supporting guides and one current
   Aeora conversion or programme page.
2. Each supporting guide links to exactly one relevant pillar and two related
   guides. It must not link to every page in the cluster.
3. Courses links to the institutional-trading guide for context; it remains the
   only commercial course destination.
4. Pinnacle links to the professional-trader career guide; it remains the only
   programme-intake destination.
5. The Research index separates dated research notes from evergreen Malaysia
   field guides so current analysis is not displaced by a large content batch.
6. The footer carries the three top-level guide paths, not the entire article
   library.

## Cannibalisation guardrails

- Do not create a separate `what-is-a-prop-desk` page while
  `/prop-desk-malaysia` is live.
- Do not create separate articles for order flow and volume profile unless their
  content or intent genuinely diverges.
- Do not publish city variants such as Kuala Lumpur prop desk or Penang trading
  career pages without a real local service, event or original evidence.
- Do not target provider rankings, payout comparisons, discount codes, live job
  listings or availability pages unless Aeora has a verified and maintainable
  editorial or operational basis for them.
